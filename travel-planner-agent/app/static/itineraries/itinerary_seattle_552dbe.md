## 🗓️ 3-Day Curated Seattle Itinerary

### Day 1: Iconic Waterfront & Downtown Delights
- **Morning (09:00 - 12:00):** Explore the historic **Pike Place Market**. Watch the famous fish mongers, visit the original Starbucks, and wander through the flower and artisan stalls.
- **Lunch (12:30 - 13:30):** Clam chowder in a sourdough bowl at *Pike Place Chowder* or fresh Dungeness crab roll at *Market Grill*.
- **Afternoon (14:00 - 17:00):** Stroll down the Seattle Waterfront to the **Seattle Great Wheel** and walk through the open-air **Olympic Sculpture Park** along Elliott Bay.
- **Evening / Dinner (18:00 - 21:00):** Head up to **Kerry Park** in Queen Anne for the iconic postcard sunset view of the Seattle skyline with Mt. Rainier in the backdrop. Dinner in Queen Anne or Belltown (try fresh Pacific salmon).

---

### Day 2: Seattle Center Icons & Cultural Highlights
- **Morning (09:30 - 12:00):** Ascend the **Space Needle** for 360-degree views from the rotating glass floor, followed by the vibrant glass art exhibits at **Chihuly Garden and Glass**.
- **Lunch (12:30 - 13:30):** Casual gourmet bites inside the *Armory at Seattle Center* or Korean fusion in Lower Queen Anne.
- **Afternoon (14:00 - 16:30):** Dive into pop culture, music history, and sci-fi at the Frank Gehry-designed **Museum of Pop Culture (MoPOP)**.
- **Evening / Dinner (17:00 - 21:00):** Board the **Washington State Ferry to Bainbridge Island** (35-min scenic crossing). Enjoy views of the illuminated skyline on your return, followed by seafood dining on the Pier (e.g., *Ivar's Acres of Clams* or *Elliott's Oyster House*).

---

### Day 3: Historic Underground & Maritime Heritage
- **Morning (09:30 - 12:00):** Explore historic **Pioneer Square** and take the entertaining subterranean **Bill Speidel's Underground Tour**.
- **Lunch (12:30 - 13:30):** Handcrafted dim sum in the nearby *Chinatown-International District* or artisanal sandwiches at *Salumi*.
- **Afternoon (14:00 - 17:00):** Head north to the **Ballard Locks (Hiram M. Chittenden Locks)** to see boats transit between Puget Sound and Lake Union, watch salmon ladders, and visit **Golden Gardens Park**.
- **Evening / Dinner (17:30 - 21:00):** Craft brewery hopping and dinner in trendy Ballard (e.g., *The Walrus and the Carpenter* for world-class oysters).

---

## 💡 Practical Travel Tips
- **Transit in Seattle:** Light rail (Sound Transit Link) runs directly from SEA Airport to Downtown in ~38 minutes for under $3.50.
- **Weather / Layers:** Seattle weather can be crisp and drizzly even when sunny; pack water-resistant outer layers and comfortable walking shoes.
- **CityPASS:** Consider the Seattle CityPASS if you plan to visit both Space Needle, Chihuly, and MoPOP for ~44% savings.
