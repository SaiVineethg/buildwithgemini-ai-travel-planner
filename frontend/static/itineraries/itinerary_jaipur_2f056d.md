## 🗺️ 3-Day Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: The Heart of the Old Walled City (Pink Heritage)
* **Morning (09:00 AM – 11:00 AM):** Visit the iconic **Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)** early for soft morning photography. Grab traditional masala chai and samosa opposite the palace at *Tattoo Cafe / Wind View Cafe*.
* **Mid-Day (11:15 AM – 02:00 PM):** Explore the majestic **City Palace Complex** (Chandra Mahal & Mubarak Mahal) and walk over to the UNESCO-listed **Jantar Mantar Astronomical Observatory**.
* **Lunch (02:00 PM – 03:00 PM):** Traditional Rajasthani Thali at *LMB (Laxmi Misthan Bhandar)* or *The Rajput Room*.
* **Afternoon & Evening (03:30 PM – 07:30 PM):** Stroll through **Johari Bazaar & Bapu Bazaar** for hand-block printed textiles, mojris, and lac bangles. Visit **Albert Hall Museum** lit up at night.
* **Dinner (08:00 PM):** Rooftop dining with views of Old City at *Handi* or *Niros* on MI Road (try authentic Laal Maas / Dal Baati Churma).

---

### Day 2: Royal Hilltop Forts & Lake Palaces
* **Morning (08:30 AM – 12:30 PM):** Drive north to **Amber Fort (Amer Palace)**. Wander through Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace) and royal courtyards. Optional: Visit the historic stepwell **Panna Meena ka Kund**.
* **Lunch (01:00 PM – 02:00 PM):** Heritage dining at *1135 AD* (inside Amer Fort) or *The Stag Restro Cafe*.
* **Afternoon (02:30 PM – 03:30 PM):** Photo stop at **Jal Mahal (Water Palace)** floating on Man Sagar Lake.
* **Late Afternoon & Sunset (04:00 PM – 06:30 PM):** Drive up into the Aravalli Hills to **Nahargarh Fort** for panoramic golden hour sunset views over the entire Pink City from *Padao Open-Air Cafe*.
* **Dinner (08:00 PM):** Heritage dinner at *Baradari (City Palace)* or *Steam at Taj Rambagh Palace*.

---

### Day 3: Cultural Immersion, Royal Gardens & Folk Evenings
* **Morning (09:30 AM – 11:30 AM):** Visit the serene **Patrika Gate** at Jawahar Circle for vibrant Rajasthani architecture murals, followed by the royal cenotaphs at **Gaitore Ki Chhatriyan**.
* **Lunch (12:30 PM – 02:00 PM):** Modern cafe lunch at *Cafe Palladio* or *Anokhi Cafe*.
* **Afternoon (02:30 PM – 05:00 PM):** Artisan block printing workshop at *Anokhi Museum of Hand Printing* or shopping at *Jaipur Blue Pottery Art Centre*.
* **Evening & Night (06:00 PM – 10:30 PM):** Immerse in traditional folk dances, puppet shows, camel rides, and grand Rajasthani dining at **Chokhi Dhani Ethnic Cultural Village**.

---

## 💡 Pro Travel Tips for Jaipur
1. **Composite Ticket:** Buy the Rajasthan Tourism Composite Entry Ticket covering Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, and Albert Hall Museum to save queue time.
2. **Best Time to Visit:** October to March (delightful winter temperatures between 15°C and 25°C).
3. **Getting Around:** App cabs (Uber/Ola) and auto-rickshaws are readily available. For Amer & Nahargarh, hiring a full-day private cab is most convenient.
